# Favicon Study Ways

Мордочка манула для «Полосатой» темы и общей иконки сайта.
Создана встроенным инструментом `image_gen`, без CLI, на основе `portrait.png`.
Исходник: `favicon-source.png`. PNG и ICO в `public` получены уменьшением исходного изображения с сохранением прозрачности; иконка Apple использует кремовую подложку.

## Промпт

Use case: precise-object-edit / logo-brand. Asset type: website favicon and application icon for Study Ways. Image 1 is the edit target: the app's established Pallas's cat mascot portrait. Turn this portrait into a compact, highly readable favicon while preserving the mascot's recognizable broad round flat face, very low rounded ears, yellow-green eyes, gray-sand fur, dark forehead stripes and warmly serious friendly expression. Show ONLY the head: remove the paws and body entirely. Front-facing symmetrical face, large bold eyes and clean chunky cheek silhouette, slightly simplified stylized 3D animated family-film shading, warm oatmeal and caramel fur with strong dark facial markings. Keep the whole head and ears in frame, centered, filling 90% of a square image, just enough transparent margin. Designed to remain recognizable at 16 and 32 pixels, reduce wispy hair detail and fine whiskers, no delicate outlines. Genuinely transparent alpha background. No lettering, text, book, frame, badge, scenery, glow, watermark, extra characters or limbs. Preserve the supplied mascot identity; create a single finished icon, not a contact sheet.
