# Манулья тема — иллюстрации

Создано встроенным инструментом image_gen (без CLI). Все три PNG имеют прозрачный фон.

- `reader.png` — манул с книгой, главный блок каталога.
- `sleeping.png` — спящий манул, боковое меню и пустой каталог.
- `portrait.png` — мордочка, знак приложения и выбор темы.

Изображения декоративные; у них пустой alt, они не перехватывают нажатия.

## Использованные промпты

### reader.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent character illustration for a friendly learning app theme. Subject: a single adorable Pallas's cat (manul), unmistakably a manul rather than a domestic kitten: extremely fluffy sandy-gray fur, wide flattened round face, tiny rounded ears set low on the sides, broad cheeks, short stocky paws, yellow-green eyes, subtle forehead stripes, thick banded tail. The manul sits on its round haunches behind an open teal-covered book, one paw resting on a page, with a comically serious but kind curious expression. Premium stylized 3D animated feature-film character, Pixar / DreamWorks-like quality, expressive face, detailed soft fur groom, gentle rounded forms, warm cinematic studio lighting with amber rim light, polished charming educational illustration. Full body and entire book visible, comfortable transparent margin, centered composition, no environment or floor, genuinely transparent alpha background, minimal soft contact shadow only. Palette: warm oatmeal gray fur, teal book, a tiny terracotta bookmark. No letters, no logos, no watermark, no clothing, no extra characters. Readable silhouette even at 300px display size.

### sleeping.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent mascot illustration for the bottom of a learning app sidebar. A single adorable sleeping Pallas's cat (manul), a fluffy round gray-sand ball with tiny low-set rounded ears, wide flat broad face, thick striped tail wrapped around its paws. It is curled up on two small closed books, eyes comfortably shut, one oversized soft paw hanging over the front edge. Premium stylized 3D animated family feature-film style reminiscent of Pixar / DreamWorks, soft plush fur strands, appealing rounded proportions, warm amber and cream lighting, soft natural shading. Full animal and books completely in frame, compact slightly landscape composition, centered on genuinely transparent alpha background with only a delicate contact shadow, no environment. Books muted teal and caramel. No letters, no logos, no watermark, no clothing, no extra characters. Keep details simple enough for a 180px sidebar illustration.

### portrait.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent small character portrait for a theme swatch and application brand badge. Close portrait of a single cute Pallas's cat (manul) looking directly at the viewer, unmistakable low-set tiny rounded ears, enormously fluffy wide cheeks, a very broad slightly grumpy flat face, big yellow-green eyes, gray-sand fur with subtle dark forehead markings. Two tiny plush front paws peek at the lower edge of the portrait, gently waving one paw. High-end 3D animated family-movie look reminiscent of Pixar / DreamWorks, charming and expressive, dense soft fur, rounded shapes, warm cinematic amber and soft cream studio lighting. Head, ears and both paws fully visible, isolated centered portrait on genuinely transparent alpha background, generous clear margin, strong simple silhouette for a 40px avatar, no border or frame. No text, no logos, no watermark, no clothes or extra subjects.
